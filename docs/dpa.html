Data Processing Agreement (DPA) Template

Template version: August 2026 — pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR

⚠️ When is this required?
This DPA is required only if you operate MultiBotV2 as a service for other organisations or server owners (i.e. you are not the sole owner/operator of the servers the bot runs on). If you run the bot exclusively on your own server for your own community, you are both controller and operator — no DPA is needed.

This is a template only — not legal advice. Have this reviewed by a qualified legal professional before use, especially for commercial deployments.

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between:

Controller: the server operator who deploys MultiBotV2 to their Discord server ("Controller")

Processor: the developer/operator who provides and maintains the MultiBotV2 instance ("Processor")

§ 1 — Subject matter and duration

The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller for the purpose of operating the MultiBotV2 Discord bot on the Controller's Discord server(s). The agreement is in force for as long as the Processor provides the bot service to the Controller.

§ 3 — Controller's obligations

  • The Controller is responsible for informing members of the server about data collection in accordance with Art. 13/14 GDPR.
  • The Controller ensures that processing of personal data through the bot has a valid legal basis under Art. 6 GDPR.
  • The Controller will promptly forward data subject requests (access, deletion, correction) to the Processor where the Processor's cooperation is needed.
  • The Controller will keep their Discord server's bot integration private or clearly labelled as community-run, so members are not misled about the data controller's identity.

§ 4 — Processor's obligations

  • The Processor processes personal data only according to documented instructions from the Controller (the bot's configured features).
  • The Processor ensures that persons authorised to process personal data are under an appropriate obligation of confidentiality.
  • The Processor implements appropriate technical and organisational security measures pursuant to Art. 32 GDPR, including encryption of sensitive values (webhook URLs, tokens) at rest.
  • The Processor assists the Controller with data subject rights (Art. 15–22 GDPR) through the bot's built-in commands (/data info, /data delete).
  • The Processor notifies the Controller without undue delay of any personal data breach (Art. 33 GDPR).
  • The Processor deletes or returns all personal data upon termination of the service relationship, at the Controller's choice.

§ 5 — Sub-processors

No other sub-processors are used. The GitHub DB-sync feature has been removed and is not available.

§ 6 — International data transfers

Processing takes place on the infrastructure of the hosting provider chosen by the Processor (default: Render.com, USA). Transfers to the USA are made on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), as applicable. The hosting provider's own privacy documentation should be consulted for details.

§ 7 — Deletion and return of data

Upon termination of the service, the Processor will delete all personal data from the bot's database within 30 days, unless the Controller requests return of data via the /bot-backup export command prior to termination. The Controller acknowledges that once data is deleted it cannot be recovered.

§ 8 — Governing law

This DPA is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction of the Processor. Disputes are subject to the competent courts in that jurisdiction.
